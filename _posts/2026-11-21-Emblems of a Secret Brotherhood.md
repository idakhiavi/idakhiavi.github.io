title: "Emblems of a Secret Brotherhood"

I’m not a socializer but I want to believe I can be good at connecting with people. I can talk to strangers, make friends easily, and usually find something in common with almost anyone. But I would always find closeness difficult because, as Saint-Exupéry wrote in The Little Prince, “words are the source of misunderstandings.”
And I think about that a lot... You can talk to a colleague, a stranger at a party, your neighbour etc, think that understand them in one way, and that you were understood clearly but that's seldom the case... I spend way too much time wondering what someone meant, what I meant, or whether we understood each other at all... it's a trip to the hell and back every single damn time.

Maybe that’s why I love it so much when someone mentions a book. I love how Milan Kundera in one of this books referred to books as “emblems of a secret brotherhood,” and that’s exactly how it feels to me. If you tell me about a book you loved, especially one that I loved too, a little bit of the awkwardness disappears. It feels like we’ve already been somewhere together... a long trip or in a past life. We’ve thought about at least one of the same things, maybe worried about it, laughed at it, or been disturbed by it. I don’t immediately think we have the same taste or that we must agree on everything. I just feel like maybe we’re already met and talked in some small way, and now we only have to get to pick up where we have left... thank to the magic of literature
